Questions

Frequently asked

Which Macs does it work on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14.2 Sonoma or later, including Sequoia, Tahoe and Golden Gate.

Does Mixwell record my audio?

No. macOS calls the permission "System Audio Recording" because Mixwell has to receive an app's sound in order to change its volume. The sound goes straight from the app, through Mixwell's volume control, to your speakers or headphones. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

Does it work with browsers, Spotify, Zoom and games?

Yes. Anything that plays sound shows up, including individual browsers. Safari and Chrome are each controlled as one app, including their tabs.

Will it slow down my Mac or add delay?

Apps you leave at 100% are not touched at all. Apps you adjust get about 14 milliseconds of extra delay, well below what you can notice in video or calls. Mixwell itself uses around 0.2% CPU in the background.

Does it work with AirPods and Bluetooth headphones?

Yes, at full quality. Mixwell doesn't use your headphones' microphone, so they stay out of the lower-quality call mode.

What happens if Mixwell quits?

Every app goes straight back to playing normally, at its usual volume, on its usual output.

How do I move it to a new Mac?

On the old Mac, open Mixwell Settings and click Deactivate under License. Then activate the same key on the new Mac. Your license works on 2 Macs at a time.

Why does macOS ask me to confirm the app the first time?

Mixwell is sold directly, not through the App Store, so macOS asks you to approve it once: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. After that it opens normally and updates itself.

Can I get a refund?

I lost my license key.

It's in your purchase email from Dodo Payments. If you can't find it, email support with your purchase email address.
